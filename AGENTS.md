# Workspace Guidelines

## Always Apply Unslop
Always apply the **unslop** rules and review pass (~/.agents/skills/unslop/SKILL.md) to all responses, documentation, and code comments.

### Core Unslop Rules:
1. **Remove AI vocabulary and puffery**: Cut words like crucial, delve, enhance, vibrant, pivotal, tapestry, testament, revolutionize, seamlessly, etc.
2. **Plain speech**: Prefer concrete words over jargon (e.g. use 'use' instead of 'utilize' or 'leverage').
3. **Cut filler and chatbot artifacts**: No sycophantic praise ('Great question!'), no repetitive filler ('In order to', 'It is important to note that').
4. **Natural style**:
   - Avoid em dashes entirely; use periods or commas.
   - Use sentence case for headings.
   - No decorative emojis in headings and bullets.
   - Avoid rule-of-three phrasing and synonym cycling.
5. **Add human voice and directness**: Be concise, direct, opinionated, and state facts and mechanisms directly.
